What the 36 gunas actually measure

Ashtakoot is a Moon-based compatibility screen. It is one input — not a replacement for the two people, their families, or a full chart reading.

Moon, not sun signs

Vedic matching uses the Moon’s nakshatra and rashi at birth — the mind and emotional temperament — rather than the Western sun sign.

Eight complementary tests

Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana, Bhakoot and Nadi together weigh temperament, instinct, mind and health.

Doshas have remedies

Manglik, Bhakoot and Nadi doshas are traditionally addressed with Mangal Shanti, Navgrah Shanti and simple lifestyle remedies.

A note on low scores. A single number can feel heavy. Our astrologers read the full chart before advising anything — and we will tell you plainly when a score is being over-interpreted.
