Booking, confirmation and changes

A booking is confirmed only after payment is authorised and a booking ID (VP-XXXXXX) is generated. You will see the ID on screen and receive it on WhatsApp along with the pandit’s name, the muhurat window and the samagri list.

The pandit is assigned from the tradition, language and speciality you selected. If the assigned pandit cannot attend, we replace them with another verified pandit of the same tradition at no extra cost, or offer a full refund if you prefer not to proceed.

For society entries, add the society name, tower and flat at checkout. We generate the gate pass and share a one-time code with the pandit one hour before the ritual. If your society requires a resident approval, our coordinator will call you in advance.

Date or time changes are free up to 24 hours before the slot, subject to pandit availability. Within 24 hours, a change is treated as a cancellation of the original slot and a fresh booking.

Pricing, GST and payment

The price shown on the booking page is the final price. It includes the pandit’s dakshina, travel within the city, the complete samagri kit, fresh flowers and the Punya-Flow collection where applicable. There is no separate cash dakshina.

Prices are quoted in Indian Rupees and are exclusive of 18% GST, which is added and shown separately before payment. The tax invoice is emailed to the address on the booking.

We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, and netbanking through a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We never store your card number or UPI PIN. Cash is accepted only for add-ons purchased on the day, and a receipt is issued.

Optional extras — additional flowers, a larger havan kund, a second pandit, or a longer vidhi — are priced before you confirm. The pandit will not ask for an unreceipted top-up mid-ritual.

Cancellation, rescheduling and refunds

More than 24 hours before the slot: free cancellation, full refund of the amount paid, credited to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Within 24 hours: the samagri and travel components are retained, and the balance is refunded. If we have already dispatched fresh flowers, the flower cost is non-refundable.

Rescheduling: one free change per booking, subject to availability. A second change attracts a ₹299 coordination fee.

If we are late: if the pandit arrives more than 15 minutes after the booked window without a prior call, the booking fee is waived for that ritual. If we cannot perform at all, you receive a full refund and a replacement pandit at no charge on a mutually agreed date.

Force majeure: for government restrictions, extreme weather, building closures or a family emergency, we will reschedule without penalty. Refunds in these cases are processed after deducting only actual, receipted costs.

Cancellation by us: if a verified replacement cannot be found, you receive a 100% refund plus a ₹500 credit toward the next ritual.

Privacy and your data

We collect only what the ritual needs: your name, phone number, delivery address, society details, the date and muhurat, the gotra and birth details you choose to enter, and your language or tradition preference.

Birth details entered into the kundli or matching tools are processed in your browser. They are not sent to a server unless you attach them to a booking or send them to the acharya desk. We do not sell, rent or barter personal data.

The assigned pandit sees only the name, phone number, address, ritual and gate instructions needed to perform the pooja. Access is removed after the ritual.

Payment data is handled by the gateway; we see only the last four digits and the payment status. You can ask us to delete your booking history by writing to rajivlochanmishra952@gmail.com; we retain invoices for the period required by tax law.

We send WhatsApp updates about the booking you placed. Marketing messages are opt-in and can be stopped by replying STOP.

GST invoices and corporate bookings

Add your GSTIN at checkout, or send it to rajivlochanmishra952@gmail.com with the booking ID. The invoice is issued before the ritual begins and carries the SAC code for religious and ceremonial services.

For corporate rituals, we support purchase orders, vendor onboarding, MSME declarations and monthly consolidated invoicing. TDS certificates can be matched against the invoice series.

NRI customers can pay in USD, GBP, AED or SGD through the gateway. The invoice is issued in INR with the applicable GST treatment stated on the document.

Bulk samagri and employee gifting orders are invoiced under the same GSTIN, with an itemised annexure for your finance team.

Samagri, flowers and returns

Every kit is checked against the ritual sequence before dispatch. If an item is missing or damaged, tell us within 24 hours with a photo and we will replace it before the muhurat or refund that item.

Sealed, non-perishable samagri and pooja ware can be returned unused within 48 hours of delivery. Fresh flowers, cut fruit, prasad, opened ghee and custom-packed kits cannot be returned for hygiene and ritual reasons.

Gemstones and rudraksha are shipped with their lab certificate. A piece can be returned unworn within 7 days if the certificate and seal are intact. Custom-set rings are non-returnable.

Punya-Flow pickup is included with all-inclusive bookings. For standalone pickups, book the add-on and keep the waste dry and segregated; our team collects within 24 hours of the request.
