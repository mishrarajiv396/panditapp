Your Vedic rashi is the sign the Moon occupied at your birth. If you do not know it, generate a free kundli — the Moon sign is shown on the first page.

How this is calculated. The Moon’s sidereal longitude (Lahiri ayanamsa) gives today’s transit sign. From your moon sign, the Moon falls in one of twelve houses; each house has a classical theme and a strength weight. Tithi, nakshatra and yoga then refine the tone.
