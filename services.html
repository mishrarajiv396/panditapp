Astrology consultation

A birth chart is a map of timing. We read it to tell you what is being tested, when the window opens, and which remedy is proportionate — nothing more, nothing less.

What we read

  • Career & profession — the tenth house, dashā periods, job change, business versus service, and the right month to move.
  • Marriage & matchmaking — a full guna-milan with Manglik, Nadi and Bhakoot checks, plus an honest note on delay or dosha.
  • Children & santaan — the fifth house, putra-karaka, and the timing of a positive phase.
  • Gemstone consultation — a stone is prescribed only after the chart confirms it, with weight, metal, finger and wearing day.
  • Palmistry & prashna — a palm reading or a specific-question horary when you do not have an exact birth time.
  • Daily, monthly & yearly horoscope — free rashifal for all twelve signs on the site, refreshed every day.

How to prepare

Keep your date, exact time and place of birth ready. If the time is unknown, tell us — we will either rectify the chart or use a prashna reading instead of guessing. The consultation is a conversation, so bring the three questions that matter most.

Consultations are available in person in Gurugram, by phone, or over video for NRI families. A written summary with the remedy and the favourable window is shared after the call.

Vastu Shastra

Vastu is the applied knowledge of the Atharva Veda — the study of direction, element and proportion. A correction should make a space calmer and more useful, not unlivable.

Home Vastu

The five elements have a natural placement: water in the north-east, fire in the south-east, air in the north-west, space in the south-west and earth at the centre. When one is blocked or inverted, the effect usually shows up as a repeated pattern — sleep, money, health, or friction between family members.

A home visit covers the entry, kitchen, bedrooms, puja room, toilets, staircases and the Brahmasthana. Where a wall cannot move, we use colour, metal strips, yantras, mirrors and planting as non-demolition remedies.

Business Vastu

For a shop, office or factory, the four things we check first are strength, finance, debt and health. The owner and directors sit in the south, west or south-west; accounts and cash sit in the north or east; and the entrance and passages are tested against the owner’s chart.

  • Strength — seating, cabin orientation and decision flow for the leadership team.
  • Finance — cash, records and the finance team placed in the north and east zones.
  • Debt trap — clearing a blocked north with running water, air and a Sri Yantra.
  • Health — balancing the fire, water and air zones that map to pitta, kapha and vata.

Every assignment ends with a room-wise report, a priority list and a matching Vastu Dosh Homa if a ritual remedy is also needed.

Anka Shastra

Numerology

Nine numbers, one birth date. Numerology reduces every date and name to a single digit, then reads the pattern — lucky days, colours, numbers, and the years that ask for caution.

It is the simplest branch of the divine sciences to begin with, and the easiest to misuse. We use it as a second opinion alongside the birth chart, not as a replacement for it. A name correction or a vehicle number should follow a full reading — never a single online total.

What the three numbers mean

  • Mulank (birth number) — the day you were born, reduced. It describes the temperament you lead with.
  • Bhagyank (destiny number) — the full date reduced. It describes the direction life keeps nudging you toward.
  • Namank (name number) — the Chaldean value of your full name. Used for spelling and business-name corrections, never on its own.

For a full reading — career, marriage, business name, house or vehicle number — book a numerology consultation. We cross-check the numbers against your chart before recommending a change.
